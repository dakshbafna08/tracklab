STEP 03 // PIT STOP TIMING

When Do You Pit?

In Formula 1, pit timing is everything. Pit too early, and your second set wears out before the checkered flag. Pit too late, and you lose seconds crawling on dead rubber.

⚡ The Pit Stop Window:

When your race engineer yells "Box, box, box!", your car dives into the pit lane for 4 fresh tires. Use the interactive slider to find the optimal crossover lap where fresh rubber overcomes the ~20s pit lane time loss.
