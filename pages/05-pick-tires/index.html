STEP 02 // TIRE ALLOCATION

Choose Your Tires

A stint is simply the continuous segment of laps you drive on a single set of tires. In Formula 1, you pick a starting tire for the race launch, and a fresh compound to switch to when you box.

⚠ The FIA Two-Compound Mandate:

In dry conditions, you must use at least two different tire compounds during the race (e.g. Soft then Hard, or Medium then Soft). You cannot stay on the same compound all race long.
