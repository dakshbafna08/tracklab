04 // Pit Stop Mathematics

Undercut early.
Or overcut late.

Every pit stop costs roughly 20 to 24 seconds in pit-lane transit. The art of strategy is knowing when fresh rubber recovers that deficit fastest.

Undercut

Box Before Your Rival

Pit one to two laps earlier onto fresh tires. Use peak mechanical grip to set purple sectors while your rival slows down on degraded rubber.

Overcut

Extend in Clean Air

Stay out when your car feels light and your tires remain below their thermal cliff, using clean air while your rival exits into traffic.
