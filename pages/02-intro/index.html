Welcome to the Pit Wall, Driver.

In Formula 1, raw speed alone doesn't win Grand Prix championships. Races are won and lost on the pit wall through tire selection, fuel management, and precise pit window timing.

01 // Rubber Science

Tire Compounds & Cliffs

Softer tires are lightning-fast off the line but degrade rapidly. Harder tires last longer but are slower. Every tire eventually hits a "cliff" where lap times drop off by multiple seconds.

FIA Rule: Must run 2 distinct dry compounds
02 // The Pit Delta

Pit Lane Time Loss

Stopping for fresh rubber is never free. At Norisring, driving through the speed-limited pit lane costs approximately 18.4 seconds. Your tire pace advantage must overcome this deficit.

Norisring Pit Loss: 18.4s fixed delta
03 // Tactical Overtaking

The Undercut

Can't pass the car ahead on track? "Box" (pit stop) a lap before them. Your brand new tires deliver an immediate 2-second pace advantage on your out-lap, jumping you ahead when they pit.

Clean Air + Fresh Rubber = Net Position Gain
